# Agriculture and Fisheries Services

- Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA) enrollment
- Securing Crop Manager fertilizer recommendation
- Fertilizer recommendation through soil analysis
- Rice seeds or fertilizer assistance
- Assistance on various agricultural farm inputs
- Request or pre-masterlisting of high-value commercial crops: citrus, banana, pineapple, cassava, coconut, and dragon fruit
- Issuance of farmer and land certification
- Fisherfolk registration
- Fishing boat registration
- Fry or fingerlings dispersal
- Application for crops, livestock, and fisheries insurance
- Distribution of vegetable seeds
- Provision of technical assistance
- Animal health care service
- Marine mammal stranding response
- Inspection of food animals and carcasses

## Notes

Some services are limited to eligible Aparri farmers, fishers, raisers, growers, or fishpond and fish-cage operators. Confirm current availability and requirements with the Office of the Municipal Agriculturist.

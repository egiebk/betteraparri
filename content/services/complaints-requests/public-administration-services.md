# Mayor, Sangguniang Bayan, and Public Administration Services

## Office of the Municipal Mayor

- Solemnization of marriage
- Issuance of Mayor's clearance or certifications
- Issuance of Mayor's endorsement or recommendation
- Vehicle services
- Action on request for oath-taking services
- Issuance or processing of travel order request

## Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Bayan

- Accreditation of civil society and non-government organizations
- Review of complaints, petitions, and requests by the Sangguniang Bayan
- Authorization for construction of cell sites by telecommunications providers
- Reclassification of lands
- Issuance of certification of posting
- Issuance of copies of ordinances and resolutions

## Office of the Municipal Administrator

- Processing of incoming correspondence covering various requests, complaints, and proposals

# Planning, Engineering, and Zoning Services

## Office of the Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator

- Issuance of locational clearance for building permit: new regular applications, renovation or interior renovation, repair, and addition
- Issuance of locational clearance for business permit, new
- Issuance of locational clearance for building permit for Special Use Permit (SUP)
- Issuance of zoning certification or land-use certification

## Office of the Municipal Engineer

- Issuance of building permit
- Issuance of electrical permit
- Issuance of demolition permit
- Issuance of fencing permit
- Issuance of occupancy permit
- Issuance of sanitary permit

# Treasury and Property Assessment Services

## Office of the Municipal Treasurer

- Issuance of Community Tax Certificate, individual
- Issuance of Community Tax Certificate, corporation
- Issuance of certification or tax clearance
- Issuance of real property tax receipt
- Payment of business tax or Mayor's Permit
- Branding and registration of large cattle
- Registration of transfer of large cattle
- Registration of weights and measures
- Payment of burial and cemetery charges
- Payment of Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit
- Collection of regulatory fees and charges
- Collection of civil registration fees
- Payment for construction of tomb at the public cemetery

## Office of the Municipal Assessor

- Application for tax declaration for newly acquired real property, transfer of ownership, subdivision, or consolidation of ownership
- Request for certified true copy of tax declaration; certificate of property holdings, no property holdings, land with improvement, or land with no improvement
- Request for notice of cancellation of assessment
- Request for cancellation of encumbrance and mortgage of real property

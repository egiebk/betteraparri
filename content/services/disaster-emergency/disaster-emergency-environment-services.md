# Disaster, Emergency, and Environment Services

## Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office

- Disaster preparedness services, including training, drills, lectures, seminars, and information and education campaigns
- Disaster prevention and mitigation services, including flood-control or drainage clearing and nuisance-tree pruning
- Response services, including emergency medical or ambulance service, search and rescue or retrieval operations, special assistance, and rescue equipment requests

## Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office

- Request for special permit for hauling garbage
- Request for permission to cut or retrieve lumber and other forest products
- Request for information and educational campaigns in schools and barangays during assemblies

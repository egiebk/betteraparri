# Social Welfare Services

The Office of the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Officer lists the following external services.

- Provision of financial assistance to individuals in crisis situation (AICS)
- Issuance of social case study report
- Issuance of certificate of indigency and IDs for solo parents and persons with disabilities (PWDs)
- Marriage counselling service
- Social welfare interventions on special cases: child abuse, violence against women and children (VAWC), and children in conflict with the law (CICL)
- Day Care Service Program, Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD)
- Supplementary Feeding Program
- Social Pension Program
- Availing of disaster relief assistance

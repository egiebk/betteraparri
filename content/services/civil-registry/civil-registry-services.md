# Civil Registry Services

## Registration and certificate services

- Timely registration of birth of a legitimate child
- Timely registration of birth of an illegitimate child
- Delayed registration of birth
- Out-of-town delayed registration of birth
- Timely registration of marriage
- Delayed registration of marriage
- Issuance of Birth Available (Form 1A), Birth Not Available (Form 1B), Birth Destroyed (Form 1C), or certified true copy of birth
- Issuance of Marriage Available (Form 3A), Marriage Not Available (Form 3B), or certified true copy of marriage
- Issuance of Death Available (Form 2A), Death Not Available (Form 2B), Death Destroyed (Form 2C), or certified true copy of death
- Timely registration of death
- Delayed registration of death
- Application for marriage license
- Filing of petition for correction of clerical error or change of name under RA 9048 and RA 10172
- Registration of court orders
- Registration of legal instruments
- Supplemental report
- Batch Request Entry System (BREQS)

# Business, Permit, and Franchising Services

## Municipal Franchising and Regulatory Board

- Issuance of Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP), new or renewal

## Office of the Municipal Mayor

- Issuance of business permit, new
- Issuance of business permit, renewal
- Issuance of Mayor's Permit for derby or pintakasi, promotional activity, or transport of cadavers, materials, goods, and products outside the municipality
- Issuance of Mayor's Permit for a motorcade or parade
- Issuance of Mayor's Permit for construction and opening of tomb, or rental of tenement at the public cemetery

## Notes

Business and permit applications have service-specific documentary and fee requirements. Confirm the current checklist with the responsible office.
